FINDINGS
F1 (address-space gap, display vs mmio.h): disp_mode's EVO_MTHD steps carry cl907d method offsets, and gf119.c reads the latched state back at 0x640414 + head*0x300 — a BAR0 window (0x640000+) that pg106_mmio_map does NOT enumerate (its PDISP block ends at 0x61ffff). The test adapts with the documented mapping BAR0 = 0x640000 + method offset and asserts non-interference on explicit ranges; a future phase should add the EVO method window to pg106_mmio_map. No undocumented hack was needed.
O1 (observation): the ACR falcon base 0x3c0000 is a documented SIM stand-in (acr_boot.h); it lies in an unenumerated BAR0 gap (pg106_mmio_block_at -> NONE), as on real hardware where nvkm_top discovers it at runtime.
