FINDINGS
F1 (resolved 2026-11-07): an earlier model wrote display methods straight to BAR0 0x640000 + method offset. On GP102 0x640000+ is the channel USER/PUT area plus the ARMED-state mirror (gp102.c gp102_disp_core_init, gf119.c:429); methods only execute from the core channel's DMA pushbuffer, which is what disp_core now models. pg106_mmio_map enumerates the window as PEVO.
O1 (observation): the ACR falcon base 0x087000 is the SEC2 base measured on a real GP106 (FINDINGS.md:160); nouveau discovers it from nvkm_top at runtime.
